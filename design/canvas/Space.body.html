Space, radius & grid

The spacing scale is not a doubling ramp — it steps 4, 8, 12, 16 and then widens to 22, 26, 34, because the screen gutter (22) and the section rhythm (26–34) needed values a strict 4/8 grid keeps missing. Radii are assigned by component class rather than by size, so a chip and a card can never accidentally share a corner.
