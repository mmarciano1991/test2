Elevation & motion

Shadows are tinted with the blue they sit on — rgba(15,43,84,…) for neutral surfaces, rgba(27,64,121,…) for anything already painted primary — so nothing casts a grey shadow onto cream. These can't be Figma variables, so they live as effect styles there and hand-authored tokens here; the two have to be kept in step by hand.

Pressed is not a smaller card shadow. It is the same colour and opacity as primary with the offset and blur pulled in tight — the button keeps its weight but drops closer to the surface, which is what makes the press read as depth rather than as a colour change.

Why the screen-expand differs

The container transform that grows a button into a screen uses a symmetric cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1) instead. The decelerate curve is ~40% expanded within a few frames, which skips the growing shape almost entirely — the symmetric curve is what lets the motion be seen at all.

Durations
--motion-fast120ms · taps
--motion-base180ms · colour, shadow
--motion-nav260ms · nav layout
--motion-expand340ms · screen open
--motion-flip480ms · card flip

All of it is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion — including the transforms on press, which are reset with !important rather than merely un-transitioned, so a reduced-motion user gets a static app rather than an instantly-jumping one.
