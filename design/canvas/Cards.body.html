Cards

Every card is white-on-cream with a cool hairline and shadow-card; what separates them is density and what the Dutch word is doing. A WordCard is a specimen — the word at 34px, given room. A SenseCard is a choice — same anatomy at 20px, with its own Add button, so picking one meaning among several costs a single tap.

SenseCard & DeinflectionCard — the same shape, two different ambiguities
Sense — one word, several meanings
de aanslag Tax

Deinflection — one form, two real words
het slot Plural
lock
Het slot van de deur is kapot.
The lock on the door is broken.

Identical anatomy on purpose. "sloten" is the plural of both slot (lock) and sloot (ditch), and there is no honest way to pick one — so the ambiguity is handed over in the same shape a multi-sense word already used. The only difference is the button's verb: Add this meaning versus Add this word, because one is a sense of the same word and the other is a genuinely different one.
