Buttons

Five button roles, each with a distinct footprint so weight is legible before colour is: primary is the widest (17px padding, radius 16), secondary is visibly tighter (13px, radius 14), and the two practice-grade buttons split a row at 15px. Every state below is a real rendered instance, not a hover you have to go hunting for.
