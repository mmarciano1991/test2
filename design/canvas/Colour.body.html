Foundations

Colour — Delft Blue

Two families do the work: a Delft blue ramp for everything structural, and a warm cream ramp for surfaces. Blues are cool and saturated, creams are barely tinted — the contrast between them is what makes the app read as porcelain rather than as a generic light theme. Primitives are never used directly in components; every component reads a role token from the bottom of this sheet.

Roles — what components actually read

A component never names a primitive. It names the job, and the job points at a primitive here — so retinting the app is an edit to this list, not a search across the codebase.
