Bottom navigation — three tabs and a FAB that collapses
With FAB
FAB collapsed — the pill grows into the space

The FAB animates its own width and margin-left to zero rather than fading in place, so the pill — which is flex: 1 — grows to fill the gap in the same 260ms. The + rotates −90° on the way out, so the exit reads as deliberate instead of as a glitch. It hides on Settings, where adding a word is moot, and on the empty dashboard, which already puts that action in front of you.
